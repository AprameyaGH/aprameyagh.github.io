# Research

I am currently exploring Geometric flows. Some of my current interests include 

- four dimensional Ricci solitons
- four dimensional ancient solutions to the Ricci flow 
- singularity formation in mean curvature flows
- long time behavior of curve shortening flow 

## Preprints

1. Immortal Curve Shortening Flow on Analytic Surfaces

3. "Asymptotic Geometry of Four-Dimensional Steady Solitons" (with Natasa Sesum) arXiv preprint, [arXiv:2603.21397](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21397) (submitted).
