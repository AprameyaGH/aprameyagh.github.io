# An Example in LaTeX

The area of a circle with radius \(r\) is

\[
A = \pi r^2.
\]

We can also write a more complicated equation:

\[
\frac{d}{dt}\gamma(s,t)
=
\frac{\partial^2 \gamma}{\partial s^2}(s,t).
\]

This is the curve-shortening flow equation.

For a multiline calculation, use:

\[
\begin{aligned}
f(x)
&= (x+1)^2 \\
&= x^2 + 2x + 1.
\end{aligned}
\]
