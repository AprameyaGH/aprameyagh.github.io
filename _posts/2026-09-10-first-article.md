# An Example in LaTeX
The area of a circle with radius $r$ is

$$
A = \pi r^2.
$$

The curve-shortening flow equation is

$$
\frac{\partial \gamma}{\partial t}
=
\frac{\partial^2 \gamma}{\partial s^2}.
$$

For a multiline calculation, use

$$
\begin{aligned}
f(x) &= (x+1)^2 \\
     &= x^2 + 2x + 1.
\end{aligned}
$$
