# ∇ Seminar - Graduate Student Analysis Seminar @ Rutgers University
## Fall 2026

∇ Seminar (read as "nabla seminar" or "Grad seminar") is a student run Analysis and Geometry seminar at the Department of Mathematics, Rutgers University. The seminar features various expository and research talks from students meeting every **Monday 4-5 PM** at **Graduate Student Lounge, Math Dept Hill 701**, Rutgers University. 

The Fall 2026 edition of the ∇ Seminar is being organized by Aprameya Girish Hebbar, Qi Ma and Anupam Nayak.

You may find the titles and abstracts for the past and upcoming talks below. 

Those who wish to receive e-mails for latest information of the seminar may subscribe to our mail-list by sending “Subscribe” to grad_analysis-join@email.rutgers.edu or you can email Aprameya at ah1531[at]math[dot]rutgers[dot]edu.
